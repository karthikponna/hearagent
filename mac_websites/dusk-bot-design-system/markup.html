what dusk bot is

text a bot what you need. it does the work on its own computer and remembers how you like things done .

give each bot a job.

one bot, one job, one chat. start with one and hire as you go.

  • researcherreads the 40 tabs so you don't. comes back with sources, a table and a straight answer.
  • inbox helperreads what came in overnight, drafts replies in your voice, leaves the three that need you on top.
  • code reviewerpicks up an issue, opens a pull request, and fixes what the review finds.
  • bookkeepermatches receipts to charges, and tells you when a subscription quietly got pricier.
  • travel plannerwatches fares, holds the good one, and checks you in the day before.
  • social media writerdrafts the week's posts from what you shipped. posts nothing until you say so.

work with many bots at once.

start with one. add another when the work grows. each has its own chat, job and memory, and they all work at the same time.

each bot gets its own computer

a browser and a desktop it signs in to and clicks through. watch it live, and take the mouse back whenever you like.

ask once and it becomes a routine

it runs on a schedule, or the moment something happens. routines run while you sleep.

every bot has a memory

it keeps notes on how you like things and who's who. every note is yours to read and edit.

put bots in a group

several bots in one thread hand work to each other. you step in when one of them needs a yes.

you can call them too. and they plug into 60+ apps.

is dusk bot really free?

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer with every feature. you only pay for dusk cloud or a work license.

what is dusk cloud?

a computer we host for your bots. your bots keep working when your laptop is off: routines run on time, day and night. nothing to set up, and each bot can have its own screen that you can watch.

which ai does it use?

the ones you already have. sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini, grok and others, or add an api key. replies go straight from your machine to them, on your plan.

which apps can a bot use?

60+ to start: gmail, google calendar, slack, notion, github, linear, figma, stripe and more. you connect an app once and pick which bots may use it. you can also add any mcp server yourself.

where does my data go?

on your computer, nowhere: bots, chats, files and memory stay on your disk. on dusk cloud they live on your own private cloud computer, and only you and your bots use it.

how is this different from a chatbot?

a chatbot answers. a bot here has a computer, signs in to your tools, runs on a schedule and remembers how you like things done.

is it safe to let bots click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch or take over at any time.

can i run it on my own server?

yes, and it is free. this is for people who look after their own servers. the own server guide has the one line to paste.
