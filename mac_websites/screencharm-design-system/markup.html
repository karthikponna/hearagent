Features

Built for creators

Record Anything

Smart Auto-Zoom

Smooth Cursor

Cursor smoothing is applied automatically after recording. Adjust size and speed to perfection. No re-recording needed.

Camera & Audio

Record your camera, microphone, and system audio alongside your screen. Reposition and resize the camera overlay to fit the frame.

Camera Layouts

Captions

Vertical Videos

Beautiful Backgrounds

Wrap your recording in a gradient, a photo, or a solid color. Make it match your brand or just look great.

Device Frames

Wrap your recording in a MacBook frame and turn a plain screen capture into a polished, presentation-ready visual.

Export or Share

Save locally as an MP4 in up to 4K UHD. Or skip the file entirely and upload to the cloud with one click. Your viewer gets a link. No account, no download, no friction.

What is the best screen recorder for Mac?
Screen Charm is a Mac screen recorder built for polished product demos, tutorials, and online courses. It records your screen, webcam, microphone, and system audio, then adds automatic zoom, cursor effects, backgrounds, device frames, and 4K export.
Can I record Mac system audio with Screen Charm?
Yes. Screen Charm can record your Mac screen, microphone, webcam, and system audio in one session, so you can capture app sounds, voiceover, and camera together.
Can I record my screen and webcam at the same time?
Yes. Screen Charm lets you record your Mac screen with a webcam overlay. You can reposition and resize the camera overlay while editing your recording.
Does Screen Charm export Mac screen recordings in 4K?
Yes. Screen Charm can export local MP4 files up to 4K, and you can also create shareable links for finished recordings.
How is Screen Charm different from other recording apps?

Screen Charm is a macOS app that makes screen recordings more engaging and informative.

Our innovative technology tracks and memorizes cursor positions and actions on your screen, allowing us to enhance recordings with special effects.

Additionally, you have the flexibility to adjust cursor size and smooth out movements, ensuring a polished and professional final video.

Which operating systems should I have for Screen Charm?
Screen Charm works only on the macOS operating system.
How do I screen record on Windows?

Screen Charm is Mac-only, but Windows 10 and 11 have built-in recorders. Press Windows + G to open the Xbox Game Bar and click the record button, or open the Snipping Tool on Windows 11 and switch to Record to capture part of the screen. For more options, see our guide to the best free screen recorders for Windows.

How do I print screen on a Mac?

Macs don't have a Print Screen key. Press Shift + Command + 3 to capture the whole screen, Shift + Command + 4 to drag over an area, or Shift + Command + 5 to open the toolbar that also records video. Screenshots save to your Desktop by default. More in our guide to taking screenshots on a Mac.

Why can't I find screen recording in Control Center?
On a Mac, screen recording lives in the Screenshot toolbar, not Control Center: press Shift + Command + 5 and choose Record Entire Screen or Record Selected Portion. On an iPhone or iPad, the Screen Recording button has to be added first: open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control, and choose Screen Recording (on iOS 17 and earlier, go to Settings > Control Center).
